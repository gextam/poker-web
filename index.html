NO-LIMIT TEXAS HOLD'EM

  • Six seats, blinds 5/10, 1,000 buy-in. The dealer button moves clockwise; the small and big blinds post before the deal (heads-up: the button posts the small blind and acts first before the flop).
  • Each player gets two hole cards. Betting on the pre-flop, flop (three community cards), turn and river. Best five of seven cards wins; ties split the pot, odd chips go to the first seat after the button.
  • No-limit: bet or raise any amount from the minimum (the size of the last bet or raise) up to your stack. An all-in for less than a full raise does not re-open the betting for players who already acted.
  • Side pots are built from each player’s contributions so you can only win what you covered. All-in hands run out automatically.
  • You have 20 seconds to act; the timer checks or folds for you. Busted opponents re-buy; use REBUY when you run out. ?tourney=1 raises the blinds every eight hands with no re-buys.

Keys: F fold · C or Space check/call · R raise · A all-in · ↑↓ adjust raise · H help · M mute. Opponents: Vic (tight-passive), Luna (loose-aggressive), Benny (calling station), Rex (maniac), Sofia (solid). Play money only — unofficial recreation.
